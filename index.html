The story

Why we built it this way.

Walk through almost any market in Chennai and you will find shops with good products, loyal customers and a phone that rarely rings from anyone new. The owners are not short of effort. They have boosted a few posts, paid for a package, and received a report full of likes.

What they wanted was simpler: more people asking, “Is this available?” So we built Magic Media around that one moment. Every ad, reel and page points to the same place, your WhatsApp, because that is where a Chennai customer actually asks, haggles and buys.

The “Magic” part is how little the owner has to carry. You send one message. We reply with a plan and a daily ad budget that fits. You get fewer decisions and more enquiries, and each enquiry is counted, so you always know what worked.

The “Media” part is why we begin on social platforms. That is where attention already is. Our job is to give it a reason to stop at your shop.

  1. The problem

    Good shop, quiet phone

    Customers walk in, but few new people find the business online. Money has gone into posts that earned likes and no calls.

  2. The message

    One WhatsApp

    The owner sends the business name and what they want to grow. No forms, no meetings to book first.

  3. The work

    A plan, then launch

    We reply with a plan and a daily budget. Ads, reels, a simple site and WhatsApp replies go live, and every new contact is saved to a sheet.

  4. The change

    Enquiries you can count

    The owner sees how many people asked, what each enquiry cost and which ad brought them. Then we adjust.
